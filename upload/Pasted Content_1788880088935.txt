Yes. We should now **freeze the product definition** enough that a designer and engineer could start building without guessing.

One important correction from the broader vision:

> **The Real Estate Execution OS is the company vision. It is NOT the MVP.**

For the MVP, I would enter through one extremely specific problem:

# REAL ESTATE REVENUE CONTROL

**The MVP finds discrepancies between what a property portfolio is contractually/billing-wise supposed to earn and what the company actually billed, collected and recorded.**

This is a much sharper starting point than trying to automate every real-estate workflow.

The larger opportunity is credible: CRE companies are actively investing in AI process automation, while data fragmentation remains a major obstacle; Deloitte found only 14% of surveyed firms believed they had well-structured data-management processes and robust privacy policies. ([Deloitte United Kingdom][1]) Meanwhile, incumbent platforms such as Yardi already cover broad property-management functions, accounting, procurement, maintenance and AI. ([Yardi][2]) So **we should not build another property-management system**. We should build the intelligence/control layer that detects what those systems miss.

---

# 1. DEFINE THE PROBLEM

## The problem

Real-estate companies have multiple sources of financial truth:

* leases
* rent schedules
* invoices
* property-management systems
* accounting systems
* bank/payment records
* spreadsheets
* service-charge records
* tenant records
* manually entered adjustments

These systems often don't agree perfectly.

The finance team therefore has to manually answer:

> **"What should we have collected, what did we actually collect, what did we record, and where is the difference?"**

Today this can require exporting spreadsheets, comparing records, checking leases, matching payments, investigating exceptions and contacting property managers.

The dangerous part isn't simply that this takes time.

It means **money can disappear inside operational complexity without anyone immediately knowing why.**

---

# 2. WHAT EXACTLY ARE WE SOLVING?

The product solves:

> **Automated revenue reconciliation and leakage detection for real-estate portfolios.**

In simple terms:

```text
WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN?
        ↓
LEASE / BILLING DATA
        ↓
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED?
        ↓
PAYMENT / ACCOUNTING DATA
        ↓
COMPARE
        ↓
FIND THE DIFFERENCE
        ↓
EXPLAIN THE DIFFERENCE
        ↓
CREATE ACTION
        ↓
TRACK RESOLUTION
```

### Example

The system discovers:

> Tenant A's lease says ₦2,000,000/month.

But:

* January billed: ₦2m
* February billed: ₦2m
* March billed: ₦1.6m

The system identifies:

**₦400,000 billing variance.**

Then investigates:

> Lease requires a 10% escalation from March.

Expected:

**₦2.2m**

Actual:

**₦1.6m**

Potential monthly leakage:

**₦600,000**

Now the product has gone beyond reporting.

It has found a problem that deserves action.

# 12. MVP EXCEPTION TYPES

Don't try to detect everything.

Start with perhaps **six**.

### 1. Underbilling

Expected > billed.

### 2. Missing billing

Expected billing exists but no invoice.

### 3. Underpayment

Invoice > payment.

### 4. Unallocated payment

Payment exists but cannot confidently be matched.

### 5. Missed escalation

Lease indicates increased rent but billing didn't change.

### 6. Duplicate/anomalous billing

Potential duplicate or suspicious transaction.

That's enough to prove the core thesis.

# 40. AI LAYER

Important distinction:

### Deterministic calculations

Should NOT depend on AI.

For example:

```text
Expected rent = 2,000,000 × 1.10
```

That's software logic.

Not an LLM.

AI is useful for:

* extracting lease terms
* interpreting clauses
* classifying documents
* explaining discrepancies
* matching ambiguous records
* summarising evidence
* identifying unusual patterns

### Principle:

> **Use deterministic software for truth. Use AI for interpretation.**

That is extremely important in financial software.

# 43. EDGE CASES

This is where amateur products die.

## No data

User signs up but hasn't uploaded anything.

Show:

> "Your portfolio isn't connected yet."

Not an empty dashboard.

---

## Bad data

Missing tenant IDs.

Show:

> 31 records cannot be reconciled because Tenant ID is missing.

Give:

**Download errors**

---

## Duplicate records

Don't blindly import duplicates.

Flag them.

---

## Conflicting information

Lease says:

₦2m.

Billing says:

₦1.8m.

Don't decide automatically.

Create exception.

---

## Payment can't be matched

Don't force it.

Create:

**Unallocated payment**

with confidence score.

---

## API failure

Never tell the user:

> "Something went wrong."

Tell them:

> "Payment data could not be synchronised. Your last successful sync was 10:42 AM."

Then:

**Retry**

---

## Duplicate API event

Use idempotency keys.

The same payment must not be imported twice.

---

## Partial import

Suppose 10,000 rows are uploaded.

9,842 succeed.

158 fail.

Don't roll back everything.

Show:

> **9,842 imported successfully. 158 require attention.**

```text
      ├── RECONCILIATIONS
      │
      └── EXCEPTIONS
               │
               ├── EVIDENCE
               ├── ACTIONS
               └── RESOLUTION
```

That's enough.

# 49. MVP SUCCESS METRICS

Don't measure:

> "Number of signups."

Measure:

### Activation

Percentage of organisations that successfully import enough data to run reconciliation.

### Time to first insight

**Signup → first meaningful discrepancy.**

Target:

**<15 minutes.**

Eventually:

**<5 minutes.**

### Detection quality

How many flagged exceptions are legitimate?

### Financial value identified

Total potential discrepancy identified.

### Resolution rate

Percentage of exceptions resolved.

### Recovery

Actual financial value recovered/confirmed.

### Automation rate

Percentage of reconciliation work performed without manual intervention.

```text

          └───────┬───────┘

          ┌───────────────┐
```
